A bit more about me.

The story behind the stack — how I got into tech, what I'm currently focused on, and where I'd like to take it next.

I go by many names, based entirely on the interesting life I’ve led. I'm Derick, I enjoy the moment something finally works after an hour of staring at an error message. That mix of frustration and payoff is basically why I got into this field in the first place.

My day-to-day sits at the intersection of software development, web technologies, databases, and networking. I like understanding systems end-to-end — from how a request travels across a network, to how it's handled by an application, to how the data behind it is stored and queried.

Outside of "official" work, I spend time building small projects, breaking my own Linux setups on purpose (and fixing them), and reading documentation for fun more often than I'd like to admit. I care about writing things that are simple, dependable, and easy for someone else to pick up later.

I'm currently open to opportunities where I can keep growing across the full stack — and get better at the networking and infrastructure side of things while I'm at it.
